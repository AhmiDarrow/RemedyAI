# chemistry-research references

- structure-representations.md — SMILES, InChI/InChIKey, SDF/MOL, CML, CAS; round-trip pitfalls with stereochemistry, tautomers, salts and charge; read before storing or comparing any structure.
- cheminformatics-rdkit.md — the standardisation pipeline, descriptors, fingerprints, similarity, and what a Tanimoto number does not mean; running RDKit in the project env.
- spectra-workflows.md — NMR, IR and MS interpretation order, integration and assignment discipline, isotope patterns, JCAMP-DX and mzML formats, what each technique can and cannot prove.
- computational-chemistry.md — choosing functional, basis set, solvation and dispersion; convergence and frequency checks; why a number from a bad geometry is still a number; reporting requirements.
- crystallography-and-cif.md — CIF anatomy, R1/wR2/GooF, completeness, checkCIF alerts, disorder and twinning, depositing with the CCDC or PDB.
- chemical-databases.md — PubChem, ChEMBL, CSD, ICSD, Reaxys, CAS SciFinder, NIST WebBook: what each holds, how to query it, and the licence terms that decide what you may redistribute.
- lab-safety-and-scope.md — hazard assessment as a procedure step: SDS, GHS, incompatibilities, containment, quench and waste; and the boundary where you decline and point at institutional review.
