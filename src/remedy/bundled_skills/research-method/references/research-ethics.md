# Research ethics, governance and credit

Ethics is part of the method, decided before collection, not a paragraph
at write-up. Where any of this applies it is an owner decision:
surface it, do not resolve it yourself.

## Review bodies

- **IRB / REC** - human participants and identifiable human data. Approval,
  or a documented exemption, before recruitment. Exemption is determined by
  the board, never self-declared.
- **IACUC / AWERB** - vertebrate animal work. Protocol approved before
  ordering animals; ARRIVE governs the reporting.
- **IBC / biosafety office** - recombinant nucleic acids, pathogens, human
  material. Containment level assigned by the committee.
- **Radiation, chemical hygiene, export control** - separate approvals with
  their own timelines.

Approval numbers go in the manuscript. Amendments are approved before the
changed work starts.

## Consent

Voluntary, informed, documented, revocable. The form states purpose,
procedures, risks, benefits, who holds the data, for how long, who may
access it, whether it is shared or reused, and how to withdraw. Children and
anyone dependent on the researchers need extra safeguards. Secondary use of
an existing dataset must fit the consent it was collected under.

## Data governance

- De-identify as early as possible; keep the key separate, access-
  controlled, and destroyed on the recorded schedule.
- Direct identifiers removed is not de-identified: dates, rare values, small
  cells and free text re-identify people. Check cell sizes before you
  publish a table.
- Know which regime applies (HIPAA, GDPR and its lawful basis, a DUA,
  Indigenous data sovereignty, institutional policy) and follow the stricter
  one. A data use agreement binds where the data may be stored, processed and
  shown, and which tools may touch it. Never move restricted data into a
  service it does not cover. Ask first.

## Dual-use

Do the field's ordinary published methodology. Decline the specifics
that give uplift for weapons, pathogen enhancement or transmissibility, toxin
production, precursor acquisition, or circumventing safeguards - and say
plainly that this is the line rather than answering partially. Point at the
governing process (IBC, biosafety officer, export control) instead of routing
around it; the owner engages it.

## Authorship and credit

Substantial contribution, approval of the final version, and accountability -
the ICMJE criteria are the usual reference; check current wording at
icmje.org. Agree the author list and order early, record contributions with
CRediT, acknowledge everyone else by name with their permission. Ghost and
gift authorship are misconduct. Funding, conflicts and in-kind support are
declared.

## Integrity

No image manipulation beyond uniform adjustments disclosed in the caption; no
removal of data outside the prespecified rule; no plagiarism, including of
your own text without citation. An error found after publication is corrected
promptly - by the owner.
