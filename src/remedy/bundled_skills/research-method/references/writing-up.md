# Writing up

## IMRaD, and what each part owes the reader

- **Introduction** - what is known, what is disputed, the gap, the question.
  Three to five paragraphs, every claim cited.
- **Methods** - enough that a stranger with the materials repeats the study.
  Past tense, specific: exact model and version of instruments, reagent
  identifiers, software with versions, the analysis as it was prespecified,
  and any deviation. This is the section reviewers use to judge the work; it
  is not the place to save words.
- **Results** - what happened, in the order the plan set. Estimates with
  intervals and n. No interpretation, no new methods.
- **Discussion** - what it means, against the literature; limitations that
  could change the conclusion; what would decide it next.
- **Abstract** - written last. Every number in it appears in the results.
  The conclusion sentence may not be stronger than the design allows.

## Figures and tables

- One message per figure; the caption states it and stands alone.
- Show the data (points, distributions), not only summaries; show
  uncertainty, and say what the bars are - SD, SE, or CI.
- Axes labelled with units; no truncated axes that exaggerate a difference;
  colour that survives greyscale and colour-vision deficiency.
- Every panel is regenerated by a named script, and the caption or a figure
  manifest records the command and the ledger run id.
- Tables: exact n per cell, effect + interval, and the test only after those.

## Reporting checklists

Use the one the design calls for, via `manuscript_check`: CONSORT for trials,
PRISMA for systematic reviews, STROBE for observational studies, ARRIVE for
animal work, MDAR as a base layer - all hosted by the EQUATOR Network. Check
the current version and wording on the issuing site rather than quoting item
numbers from memory. The output is evidence found for items; a human confirms
compliance.

## Numbers and honesty in the text

- Significant figures match the precision of the measurement.
- Percentages carry their denominator.
- No number appears in the text that is not produced by a recorded run.
- Exploratory findings keep the word "exploratory" in the sentence that
  reports them.
- Hedge where the evidence is thin, and be plain where it is not. Both
  directions of miscalibration are failures.

## Front and back matter

Author contributions (CRediT taxonomy), funding, conflicts of interest, data
availability (repository + accession/DOI, or the reason it is restricted),
code availability (repository + commit or archived DOI), ethics approval
number and consent statement where people or animals were involved.

## Finish

`manuscript_build` until it compiles clean, then `cite_check(resolve=True)`
until PASS, then `cite_export(only_cited_in=...)` for a minimal bib. Nothing
is submitted, posted or shared without the owner.
