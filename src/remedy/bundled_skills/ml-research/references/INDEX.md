# ml-research references

- splits-and-leakage.md — grouped, temporal and nested splits; the leakage taxonomy and how to hunt each kind; read before any training run.
- baselines-and-ablations.md — what counts as a strong baseline, equal-budget comparison, ablations that isolate exactly one change.
- variance-and-seeds.md — sources of run-to-run variance, how many seeds, reporting a spread, paired tests across seeds.
- evaluation-design.md — choosing the metric from the decision, thresholds, calibration, subgroup and slice performance, uncertainty on the metric.
- hyperparameter-search.md — search strategy, budget accounting, tuning the baseline too, reporting the space and the winning config.
- benchmark-reporting.md — result tables that support the claim, fair comparison to prior work, contamination, model cards and datasheets.
- reproducibility-checklist.md — what must be recorded and shipped for the result to be rerunnable; artifact and environment capture.
