# social-science references

- sampling-and-generalisation.md — target population, frame, probability vs convenience samples, weights, non-response and attrition, planning n; read before quoting any prevalence or writing the generalisation sentence.
- survey-and-instrument-design.md — choosing a validated instrument, item wording, response scales, order effects, attention checks, piloting; read before writing or editing a questionnaire.
- measurement-validity.md — content/construct/criterion validity, factor structure, measurement invariance, alpha vs omega, test-retest, inter-rater; read whenever a score stands in for a construct or groups are compared.
- causal-inference-toolkit.md — DAGs and adjustment sets, colliders, difference-in-differences, instrumental variables, regression discontinuity, matching and propensity scores, sensitivity analysis; read before any causal claim from non-randomised data.
- panel-and-clustered-data.md — fixed vs random effects, clustered standard errors and few-cluster corrections, within vs between effects, panel attrition, repeated-measures designs; read when observations share an error term.
- preregistration-and-replication.md — what a preregistration must pin down, deviation logs, open data and materials, multiverse and specification-curve analysis; read before data collection and before writing the results section.
- qualitative-methods.md — approach selection, purposive sampling and stopping rules, codebooks and audit trails, multiple coders, reflexivity, reporting and mixed methods; read when the data is interviews, focus groups or observation.
- human-subjects-ethics.md — ethics review, consent content, deception and debriefing, de-identification, data-protection regimes and the manuscript ethics statement; read before recruitment and before sharing any dataset.
