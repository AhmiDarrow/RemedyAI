# Mechanical testing and Weibull

Tensile, hardness, fatigue, creep and fracture toughness results are tied to
specimen geometry, conditioning, strain rate and the standard (body, number,
year). Report n, the estimator, and dispersion with every mean.

Brittle strength is a distribution. Fit Weibull; report the modulus,
characteristic strength, interval and specimen count. Size of the specimen
matters (weakest-link). Use `power_analysis` before the test.

Do not drop outliers because they moved the p-value.
