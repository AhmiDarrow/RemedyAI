# Research — papers, analysis, citations

Remedy has no research feature in this build.

There is no research mode, no research knowledge packs, and no tools for
literature search, citations, statistics, analysis ledgers or manuscripts. An
older version described these. They are not in the current runtime, so this
manual no longer lists them.

## What you can do today

Research work still goes through the normal tools:

- **Web.** `web.search` and `web.fetch` look things up and read pages. See
  [Free providers](15-free-providers.md) and [Security and data](04-security-and-data.md)
  for how web access is controlled.
- **Files and shell.** She can read your files and, when you allow it, run
  scripts you already have (R, Python, Quarto and so on) with the shell tool.
  She does not install those for you.
- **Skills.** You can add your own procedure packs. See [Skills](07-skills.md).

Treat any number she gives you from memory, and any citation she has not
fetched, as unchecked. Ask her to open the source.
